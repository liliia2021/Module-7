See the World (Побачити світ)

Module 7 Check Out Test · 11 points

Name and group are not graded. Drag the items (or tap an item, then tap the place where it goes). On a computer you can drag; on a phone, tap.

Task 1. Choose 5 words that name places to stay during a trip.

1 point (0.2 per correct word). Some words do not fit.

Task 2. Complete the story using the given words and phrases.

2 points (0.2 per gap).

Task 3. Put the words in order to form sentences in the Passive Voice.

3 points (0.75 per sentence). Tap a word to add it to the sentence; tap it again to remove it. Capital letters and full stops are not needed.

Task 4. Read the text and choose the correct answer for each gap.

2 points (about 0.33 per gap).

Task 5. Transform into the Passive Voice. Keep the tense.

3 points (1 per sentence). Build the passive sentence from the words (some words are extra).
